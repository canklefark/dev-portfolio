# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering managers and leads evaluating DevOps candidates during a hiring process. Technical audience, design-aware. Reviewing multiple candidates in one sitting — they notice what's different about a portfolio, not just what's competent.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Zeik's infrastructure work and career transition from design to DevOps. Goal: land a role doing serious platform, SRE, or DevOps engineering. Success is a hiring manager forwarding the URL, or an interview invite.

## Brand Personality

Raw, stripped, taut. Honest about the work. No marketing language. Motorsport is a real part of life — present as a faint watermark in naming and data labels, not as a theme park.

## Anti-references

Marketing websites. SaaS landing pages. Gradient-heavy DevOps tool dashboards. Generic dark-mode developer portfolio with teal accents and card grids. Heavy racing theming that reads as a costume.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint as confidence**: Stripped design signals expertise. Decoration signals insecurity.
2. **Motorsport as texture, not costume**: Racing references live in naming conventions and status labels. Not in hero widgets, giant numbers, or overbearing metaphors.
3. **Swiss rigor**: Grid-disciplined. Strong type hierarchy. Color is a tool, not atmosphere.
4. **Show, don't tell**: Let the project work carry the weight. No copy that explains what the reader can already see.
5. **Honest logbook tone**: Write the way the logbook entries read — direct, specific, no filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All three mockups respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Light/dark toggle preserved across all variants.
